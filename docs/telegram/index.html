CAPABILITIES

What you can do from Telegram.

Everything runs locally inside a dedicated super-desktop telegram-bot child process supervised by the daemon — whether the overlay is hidden, open, or running headless.

🗂

Live Dashboard + 1 card = 1 Topic

A pinned General dashboard shows every card plus provider Usage limits. Each running card gets its own color-tagged topic with a live status header and typing… indicator while working.

🚦

Live statuses & quiet 👍 acks

Pinned headers edit silently in place and topic names show debounced status dots (🟢 🟡 ✅ 🔴 ⚪ ⚫), while prompts in Forum Topics acknowledge with a clean 👍 reaction.

📺

Live output & smart alerts

Tap 📺 Live (/live) to watch an auto-updating <pre> block while an agent runs. Finished and Waiting alerts include trailing output snippets above one-tap buttons.

📎

Files, PDF pages & text pager

📎 Files (/files) filters Images, Docs, and Links with pagination, sends photos directly, previews PDF pages as images, pages long redacted text files inline, and opens public URL buttons.

⌨

Quick Keys & slash shortcuts

Tap ⌨ Keys (/keys) for Yes, No, 1–3, arrows, Tab, ⇧Tab, Esc, Ctrl-C, and agent shortcuts like /resume, /compact, and /clear.

📷

Bounded screen & history

Inspect live screen state with /screen or scrollback with /history. ANSI escapes are stripped and secrets masked before leaving your PC.

🚀

Launch & close sessions

Start new agents in remembered project folders using /new's 2-step inline keyboard wizard, or close finished cards cleanly with /close + confirmation.

🖼

Send photos, files & voice

Drop a screenshot, document, or voice clip directly into a card's topic — staged privately outside workspaces, never saved to random repos.

🌐

Zero ports, headless-ready

Uses outbound HTTPS long-polling to api.telegram.org. Combine with Settings → System launch to control a PC with no display logged in.

STEP BY STEP

Setup in four steps.

Takes about two minutes. You stay the owner of your bot token, your Telegram pairing, and every permission switch.

1

Telegram Create your own bot via @BotFather

  1. Open Telegram and message the official verified @BotFather bot with /newbot.
  2. Pick a display name (for example My Super Desktop) and a unique username ending in bot (for example my_superdesktop_bot).
  3. Copy the bot token (looks like 123456789:ABC...) and keep it private.

No extra @BotFather settings are needed: group invites work out of the box, and making the bot Admin in your Forum group lets it read topic messages while keeping Group Privacy Mode on.

2

PC Save the token & enable the worker

  1. Press Super + Shift + Q → open ⚙ Settings → Telegram.
  2. Paste your @BotFather token and click Save & verify (verifies directly with api.telegram.org and shows your bot's @username).
  3. Turn on Enable Telegram bot. Nothing runs until you switch it on.
3

PC + Telegram Pair your Telegram account

  1. Click Pair Telegram account on your PC to generate a single-use t.me/<bot>?start=… invite link and QR code.
  2. Open the link in Telegram and tap Start.
  3. Check that the 6-digit code matches on both screens, then click Approve on your PC. Access locks to your numeric Telegram user_id.
4

Telegram Link a 2-member Forum group (optional, recommended)

  1. Create a new private group with only you and your bot (2 members max — any extra member is refused automatically for privacy).
  2. In Group Settings, enable Topics.
  3. In Administrators, promote your bot to Admin with Manage Topics (and optionally Pin Messages so each topic pins its live status header silently).
  4. Open the group's General topic and send /linkforum. Every open card immediately gets its own live topic.

TRUST MODEL

Private control, honest limits.

Your bot, your PC, zero developer server: Bot credentials and state stay owner-only (0700/0600) on your Linux machine. Nobody else can pair without physical approval of a single-use invite + matching 6-digit code on your desktop, and any Forum group with more than 2 members is refused automatically.
Telegram Bot API is not end-to-end encrypted: Messages, images, and terminal output sent through a Telegram bot pass through Telegram's servers (and Telegram clients). Keep Redact common secrets enabled, leave unneeded switches off, and never send secrets to the chat. When you want direct device-to-device TLS with no third party at all, use the Android / iPhone / iPad app over LAN or Tailscale. Read the full Security model.

Refused: this group has N members…Remove everyone except you and your bot so the group has at most 2 members, then run /linkforum again.
Promote this bot to Administrator…In Group Settings → Administrators, make the bot Admin with Manage Topics (and optionally Pin Messages).
Bot replies that an action is disabledEnable the corresponding switch under ⚙ Settings → Telegram → Permissions (for example Read terminal output for 📎 Files and /screen).

Privacy policy for iPhone and iPad

Applies to the SUPER DESKTOP app for iPhone and iPad (com.superdesktop.ios), with its Home Screen widgets and share extension. Last updated 3 October 2026.

In short: SUPER DESKTOP has no accounts, no servers of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not receive, collect or store any of your data.

Your iPhone or iPad and your computers talk directly to each other, over your own network or Tailscale, and everything you type or see stays between them.

Who we are

SUPER DESKTOP is made by Dmytro Gladkyi, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). The app works with SUPER DESKTOP for Linux, which runs on your own computers and is open source at github.com/gladimdim/super-desktop.

What the app handles, and where it goes

InformationWhere it livesSent to us?
Pairing details for each of your computers: its name and address, a pairing token, and the pinned certificate that identifies it In the iOS Keychain, for that device only: not synced to iCloud Keychain and never moved to another device. No
Terminal output, the prompts you type, drafts, images and files you attach, file names and folder paths Sent only to the computer you chose, over a certificate-pinned encrypted (HTTPS/WSS) connection. Unsent drafts are kept on your device in a protected file excluded from backup, and terminal output only in memory while the app runs. Items you share to SUPER DESKTOP from another app wait in the app's own storage until you pick a session or cancel. No
App settings: theme, card colours, widget and usage-card choices, notification choices On your device only. They can be part of your own iCloud or computer backup, which we never receive. Home Screen widgets read a summary of your sessions that the app keeps on the device. No
Free-trial day count and cached Pro status In the device's Keychain. The App Store keeps your free-trial item and your purchase with your Apple Account. Neither is sent to us. No

Terminal output and prompts can contain whatever your AI coding tools show or you type, including source code. That content travels only between your own devices. What the AI tools running on your computers then send to their own providers is governed by those tools and providers, not by SUPER DESKTOP.

Purchases

The app offers a one-time optional purchase, SUPER DESKTOP Pro, and a free 10-day trial, both as App Store in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple. Apple processes your payment and tells the app whether you own them. We never see your payment details, name or Apple Account, and the app has no SUPER DESKTOP account. Apple's handling of that information is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Local Network: to find your computers on the same Wi-Fi network (Bonjour) and connect to them. The app connects only to computers you paired.
  • Camera: only when you choose to scan a pairing QR code. Camera images are read on the spot to decode the code; they are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Notifications: for completion alerts, if you turn them on. They are created on your device; there is no push service of ours.
  • Background app refresh: lets iOS wake the app now and then to check your computers for finished agents, if completion alerts are on.
  • Photos and files: the app can only read a photo or file you explicitly pick or share to it, using the system pickers and share sheet. It does not browse your library or storage.

The app does not read your contacts, messages, microphone, location or health data.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • No crash-reporting or usage-tracking service run by us.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps and websites.
  • No selling, sharing or renting of data. We have none to share.

App Store Connect may show us aggregate technical statistics about the app, such as installs and crash rates. They come from Apple, only if you agreed to share analytics with app developers in your device settings. We do not receive them per person.

Demo mode

The app has a demo mode with two pretend computers that live inside the device. It makes no network connections and sends nothing anywhere.

Your control and deleting data

  • Remove a computer in Settings → Connections to delete its pairing details from your device. To revoke a device's access, remove it in SUPER DESKTOP's Settings → Connections on the computer.
  • Deleting the app deletes its files and settings, including its widgets' data and anything waiting from the share sheet. iOS may keep the app's Keychain items after it is deleted: your computers' pairing details and the free-trial day count, so a reinstall finds them again and does not restart the trial. To forget your computers for good, remove them in Settings → Connections before deleting the app. Keychain items never leave the device.
  • Restore purchase brings the trial item and Pro back from the App Store.
  • Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing to request from us or to erase on our side.

Children

SUPER DESKTOP is a developer tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. A change that affects what the app collects would also be described in the app's release notes.
